# File tools — opportunity review

Review date: 2026-10-24. This is a research note. It does not approve a PDF product.

## Evidence already collected

Phase 2 Google Trends, web search, past 12 months, exact terms, collected 2026-10-24:

- United States, one chart: merge PDF average 33, PDF compressor 17, percentage calculator 74.
- United States, a rescaled chart: JPG to PDF 54 and split PDF 40 beside percentage calculator at 63 on that chart. Rotate PDF was 6. Those indexes cannot be pasted onto the first US chart.
- Canada, India, France, Spain, Mexico, and Saudi Arabia comparisons were led by a PDF merge or compress term relative to the other terms on the same chart. Those indexes are not monthly volume and are not comparable across countries.
- The US results pages opened for “merge pdf”, “jpg to pdf”, and “pdf compressor” showed Adobe, iLovePDF, Smallpdf, and other established tools. Related searches on the compressor page included 200KB, 100KB, 50KB, and 1MB.

Monthly search volume, keyword difficulty, CPC, and publisher RPM are unknown. No non-US results page was opened in Phase 2. Belgium, Switzerland, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, the UAE, Egypt, Kuwait, and French-speaking Canada were not in the Trends sample.

## Demand questions that remain open

| Question | Status |
| --- | --- |

| Publisher RPM for a PDF page | Unknown |
| Whether local-only processing is absent from the results | Unverified. One JSON result claimed in-browser processing. The PDF pages were not reopened for this review |

## Product gaps that are not yet opportunities

People search for a target file size. Phase 2 recorded that pattern. A structural PDF rewrite does not deliver a promised 100KB or 1MB file. Selling that promise is a rejected product, not a gap to fill with a vague compressor.

Password removal remains out of scope.

## Decision input

Relative interest outside the US is the strongest file-tool signal in the current research, and it is still an incomplete signal.
