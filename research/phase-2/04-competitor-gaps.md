# Competitor and results-page gaps

**Collected:** 2026-10-24.  
**Scope:** the Google results pages in `03-serp-analysis.csv`, plus Phase 1 page reads where those domains reappeared. This is not a backlink study and not a full landing-page audit.

## Verified from this pass

**Google answers “percentage calculator” on the results page.** An interactive calculator sits above the organic list. Organic sites still rank, including percentagecalculator.net, calculator.net, DadsWorksheets, Calculator Soup, and Omni Calculator. A new page can be useful for the variants Google’s widget does not cover. It should not be planned as the owner of the head query.

**Word counting is a specialist category, not an empty one.** wordcounter.net is first and has sitelinks, including a character counter and a reading-level link. writermag.com’s title already combines a word counter with reading time. Grammarly and QuillBot rank as well. The gap is not “nobody built this.”

**In-browser JSON formatting is already advertised.** json-indent.com’s result title says “100% In-Browser.” jsonformatter.org has sitelinks to related formatters. A privacy claim is not a unique angle until the network panel is checked, and it is not unique even then.

**PDF head terms in the US results are product brands.** Merge, JPG-to-PDF, and compress are led by Adobe, iLovePDF, and Smallpdf, with PDF24, Canva, Drawboard, and others also present. Phase 1 established that iLovePDF and Smallpdf upload files and that PDF24 compresses images on its servers. This pass did not retest those products. It did confirm they occupy the queries.

**Target file size is part of the compressor query, not a side note.** Related searches under “pdf compressor” included 200KB, 100KB, 50KB, and 1MB. JPG-to-PDF related searches included 300KB and 500KB. The AI Overview said text-only PDFs shrink very little. That matches the pdf-lib limitation recorded in Phase 1: a structural rewrite is not image recompression.

**UK “vat calculator” is a calculator query.** The observed list is vatcalculators.co.uk, vatcalculator.co.uk, vatulator.co.uk, Wise, vatcalconline.com, KashFlow, calculator.net, and Xero. One title already says add or remove 20% VAT. GOV.UK was not in that list. A cited, dated rate is good practice. It was not observed as an empty spot on the page.

**Compound interest is a finance-publisher query.** investor.gov is first. NerdWallet, Moneychimp, Bankrate, and The Calculator Site follow. An ad-supported new domain is a weak fit for that set even though US relative interest is high.

## Still a hypothesis

- Local-only PDF processing would win clicks. The results pages do not show that users prefer it, and several incumbents already offer a free path.
- A long-tail such as “pdf compressor to 200kb” or “percentage difference calculator” has a weaker page than the head term. Those pages were not opened.
- France, India, Mexico, Spain, and Saudi Arabia are easier PDF markets because Trends interest is high there. Their Google results were not collected. High interest and an easy ranking are different claims.
- UK VAT pages are thin. Only titles were read.

## Not found this pass

No referring-domain counts, no keyword difficulty, and no competitor revenue. Do not infer either from the brands on the page.
